# Kadraj — HEIC Viewer & Converter

Open iPhone HEIC photos on any computer and convert them to JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF.
Everything runs in the browser on your own device; photos are never uploaded.

https://erenbaris838.github.io/kadraj/

HEIC decoding uses [libheif-js](https://www.npmjs.com/package/libheif-js) (libheif and libde265, LGPL-3.0),
loaded unmodified at runtime.
